4 Pillars We Assess Through Natural Chat

Behind the scenes, PulseCoach AI translates your casual conversation into structured fitness and nutrition parameters—no spreadsheets or calorie math required.

1. Lifestyle & Recovery Rhythm

We ask about your work hours, sleep quality, and daily stress levels so we never prescribe an unrealistic 6-day workout split to a busy professional.

2. Flexible Nutrition Preferences

Instead of rigid meal plans, our AI identifies your eating habits, cultural food preferences, and non-negotiable treats to build a sustainable calorie deficit.

3. Movement History & Injury Guardrails

By chatting about past sports or joint discomfort (like lower back or knee sensitivity), the AI filters out high-risk exercises before your first session.

4. Realistic Time & Equipment Budget

Whether you have 20 minutes with dumbbells at home or 60 minutes at a full commercial gym, we tailor volume and intensity to fit your actual life.
